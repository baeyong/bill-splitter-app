Tabbly — Support

Split the bill from a photo of the receipt. iPhone and iPad.

Need help? Email tabblyhelp@gmail.com. If a receipt scanned wrong, a screenshot of the receipt and what Tabbly showed helps a lot.

Common questions

A scan got an item or price wrong.

Scans are read by AI and can occasionally misread small or faded print. Every line can be fixed as you assign it, and See all shows the whole bill laid out like the receipt so you can check the totals. Tap the photo to zoom in and compare.

It says I've used today's scans.

There's a daily scan limit to keep the service running. It resets the next day. You can still add items by hand in the meantime.

Where are my receipts stored?

Only on your phone. There are no accounts, so nothing is synced or backed up by Tabbly. Deleting the app deletes your saved receipts.

How do I stop photos being sent?

In the app, go to Settings and turn off Send photos to Claude. You'll be asked again before any future scan. Everything else works without scanning.

Why don't the tax or tip match my state's rate?

When a scanned receipt prints its tax or tip, Tabbly can use those exact amounts for that bill. Otherwise it uses the default rate from Settings, which you can change.

Privacy

Read the privacy policy. In short: everything stays on your phone except the receipt photo, and only when you choose to scan.
