Tabbly — Privacy Policy

Effective October 17, 2026 · Applies to the Tabbly iPhone and iPad app, available in the United States.

The short version: Tabbly keeps everything on your phone. The one exception is receipt scanning: if you choose to scan a receipt, that photo is sent to Anthropic's Claude AI to read the items, and only after you've agreed.

There are no accounts, no ads, no tracking, and we don't sell or share your data.

What stays on your phone

Everything you enter — people's names, items, prices, tax and tip settings, saved receipts, and notes — is stored only on your device. We can't see it. Deleting the app deletes it.

What leaves your phone, and only when you scan

Receipt scanning is optional. You can always add items by hand instead. The first time you tap Scan receipt, the app asks for your permission before anything is sent. When you scan, the app sends:

  • The receipt photo. A receipt can show things like the restaurant's name and address, the date and time, and sometimes the last digits of a card or a server's name.
  • A random install ID. A random code created the first time you use the app. It isn't linked to your name, your Apple ID, or your device's identifiers. It's kept in your iPhone's Keychain, so it stays the same if you delete and reinstall the app. It's used only to apply the weekly scan limit.

As with any internet request, your phone's IP address is visible to the server that receives it.

Who handles it

  • Tabbly's scan server (run on Cloudflare) receives the photo and passes it straight to Anthropic. It does not store or log the photo. To enforce scan limits, it keeps a count of scans per install ID for the current week, and per hashed (scrambled, one-way) IP address for the current day. Older counts are deleted.
  • Anthropic, the maker of Claude, reads the photo and returns the list of items. Under Anthropic's Commercial Terms, Anthropic may not train its models on this data. Anthropic deletes it within 30 days, except where it needs to keep it longer to enforce its usage policy or to comply with the law. See Anthropic's data retention article.
  • Cloudflare hosts the scan server and, like any web host, processes the connection to deliver it. See Cloudflare's privacy policy.

We don't use this information for anything else, and we don't sell it or share it with anyone else.

Your choices

  • Don't scan: every feature works without scanning; you can add items by hand.
  • Turn scanning off: in the app, go to Settings → Send photos to Claude. You'll be asked again before any future scan.
  • Delete your data: deleting the app removes everything stored on your phone except the random install ID in the Keychain, which holds nothing about you. Tabbly's server keeps nothing beyond this week's scan counts.

Children

Tabbly isn't directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

If we change how Tabbly handles data, we'll update this page and its effective date. If a change means more data leaves your phone, the app will ask for your permission again.
